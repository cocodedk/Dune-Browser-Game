# Stage 17 — Prescience L2 and L3

**Phase:** 3 · **Depends on:** 16 · **Est. tasks:** 2 · **Builder:** Sonnet
**Status:** TODO — **OUTLINE ONLY, spec before building**

## Goal

Convert the prescience field modelled in Stage 10 into real capability. These are
quality-of-life powers that also change the strategic picture.

## Sketch

- **L2 "Farspeech"** — remote dialogue and remote task reassignment with recruited
  characters. Earned in Act 2 by ritual with charisma ≥ 50. This is the single biggest
  reduction in busywork in the game: no more flying somewhere just to change a task.
- **L3 "Foresight"** — raid warnings three days ahead, and a global spice-density
  overlay. Earned in Act 3 after two forts are destroyed.

## Design caution

L2 removes travel from most of the loop. If flight (Stage 12) is the signature moment,
L2 is what retires it — which is correct pacing for a late game, but the spec pass must
confirm the game still has texture once the player stops flying.

## Open questions for the spec pass

- Is remote reassignment unlimited, or rate-limited to preserve some spatial cost?
- Does the L3 overlay make prospecting obsolete? If so, gate it to already-explored
  regions.
