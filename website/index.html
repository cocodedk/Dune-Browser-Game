Core Gameplay

Command the Sands

Six interlocking systems recreate the full complexity of Dune politics, economy, and survival.

⚗️

Spice Harvesting

Deploy harvesters across the Arrakeen desert to extract precious Melange. Manage production quotas, protect your crawlers from Sandworm attacks, and balance spice reserves against mounting expenditure.

⚔️

Faction Politics

Navigate treacherous allegiances between the Great Houses, the Spacing Guild, and the Bene Gesserit. Every alliance carries a price. Every betrayal echoes across generations.

🏜️

Fremen Alliance

Earn the deep desert trust of Arrakis's native people. Learn their ways, share their water discipline, and unlock Fedaykin warriors whose knowledge of the sands is unmatched.

🏛️

House Management

Oversee House Atreides — troops, Lasguns, ornithopters, and diplomatic envoys. Every strategic decision ripples through your House's honour, wealth, and military readiness.

🗡️

Political Intrigue

Broker secret deals, spread disinformation, bribe Imperial officials, or commission Mentats to outthink your rivals. In Arrakis, the sharpest weapon is a whisper.

🌐

Browser-Based

No installation required. Open your browser, and the sands of Arrakis await. Built with React, TypeScript, and Vite for a fast, seamless experience on any device.

Getting Started

Your Path to Power

Three steps stand between you and dominion over Arrakis.

  1. I

    Land on Arrakis

    Open the game in your browser — no sign-up required. House Atreides has arrived. Your sietch is established. The desert stretches before you, ancient and indifferent.

  2. II

    Harvest & Fortify

    Deploy your spice harvesters, build defensive positions, and begin forging ties with Fremen scouts. Accumulate Melange to fund your armies and buy favour with the Guild.

  3. III

    Dominate the Imperium

    Use turn-based strategy, political manoeuvring, and calculated violence to crush rival Houses and claim the Ducal seat. The spice must flow — and it must flow to you.
